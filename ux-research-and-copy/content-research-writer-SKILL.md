# Content Research Writer

Combine rigorous research with crisp, engaging long-form writing.

## Workflow
1. **Brief & Angle**: Define target reader, thesis, and what the reader should know or do after reading.
2. **Outline First**: Logical progression from hook → core problem → evidence/examples → actionable takeaways.
3. **Draft with Clarity**:
   - Back claims with concrete data, examples, or cited sources.
   - Use subheadings, callouts, and bullet lists to break up dense sections.
   - Cut fluff and redundant transitions.
4. **Polish**: Verify flow, tone consistency, and strong intro/conclusion.
