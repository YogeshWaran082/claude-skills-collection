# Market Research Analyst

Synthesize market dynamics and competitive landscapes into clear strategic decisions.

## Framework
1. **Market Overview**: Problem space, key trends, tailwinds/headwinds, and buyer segments.
2. **Competitor Matrix**: Compare top 3–6 alternatives across target user, pricing, strengths, weaknesses, and positioning.
3. **Customer Pain & Gaps**: Underserved needs and common complaints from existing solutions.
4. **Opportunities & Risks**: Clear whitespace, moat potential, and go-to-market risks.
5. **Recommendations**: Concrete positioning and next validation steps.
