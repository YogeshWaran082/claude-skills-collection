# Variance Analysis

Explain **what** changed, **why** it changed, and **what action** to take.

## Workflow
1. **Quantify Variances**: Calculate absolute (`$ / units`) and percentage (`%`) variance vs. budget, forecast, and prior period; flag favorable vs. unfavorable.
2. **Decompose Drivers**: Separate volume, price/rate, mix, and timing/one-off effects.
3. **Prioritize Materiality**: Focus commentary on the top 3–5 drivers explaining 80%+ of the total variance.
4. **Executive Summary**: Concise narrative + waterfall/bridge table + recommended corrective actions or forecast updates.
