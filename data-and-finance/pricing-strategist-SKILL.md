# Pricing Strategist

Align pricing and packaging with customer value and healthy unit economics.

## Framework
1. **Value Metric**: Identify the unit that scales naturally with customer value (seats, usage, revenue, workspaces).
2. **Packaging (Good / Better / Best)**:
   - **Starter**: Low-friction entry for core persona.
   - **Pro (Anchor/Recommended)**: Core value + team collaboration for target ICP.
   - **Enterprise**: Security, compliance, SLA, and custom limits.
3. **Economics & Guardrails**: Check gross margin, CAC payback, annual vs. monthly discount (typically 15–20%), and clear upgrade triggers.
4. **Rollout Plan**: Grandfathering/migration strategy for existing customers and pricing page clarity.
