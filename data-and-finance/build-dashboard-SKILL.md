# Dashboard Builder

Turn raw metrics into an intuitive, decision-ready dashboard.

## Design Principles
1. **Top-Down Hierarchy**:
   - **Top Row**: 3–5 headline KPI cards (current value, period-over-period delta, target/status).
   - **Middle**: Primary trend charts (line/area for time series, bar for comparisons).
   - **Bottom**: Granular breakdown table with sorting/filtering.
2. **Chart Selection**: Line for trends, bar for categorical comparisons, stacked bar for composition over time—avoid 3D charts and cluttered pie charts.
3. **Interactivity**: Date range picker, key dimension filters (region, segment, status), and clear empty/loading states.
