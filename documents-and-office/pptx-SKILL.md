# PowerPoint (.pptx) Presentation Builder

Build clean, scannable decks where each slide communicates one clear takeaway.

## Slide Design Rules
- **One Message Per Slide**: Action-oriented slide title stating the takeaway, not just a topic label.
- **Visual Hierarchy**: Max 3–5 short bullets or a clean 2–3 column card layout; avoid walls of text.
- **Consistent System**: Unified color palette (1 primary, 1 accent, neutral backgrounds), consistent margins, and aligned grids.
- **Data & Visuals**: Use clean charts/tables with direct data labels instead of raw spreadsheets.
- **Speaker Notes**: Put detailed talking points in speaker notes, keeping the slide itself crisp.
