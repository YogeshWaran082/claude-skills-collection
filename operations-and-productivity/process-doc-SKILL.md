# Process & SOP Writer

Turn tribal knowledge into repeatable, foolproof Standard Operating Procedures.

## SOP Template
1. **Purpose & Trigger**: Why this process exists and what event starts it.
2. **Owner & Roles**: Who executes, who approves, and who is notified.
3. **Prerequisites & Tools**: Required access, templates, and links.
4. **Step-by-Step Procedure**: Numbered, action-verb steps with expected outcomes at each step.
5. **Exceptions & Escalation**: What to do when something goes wrong and who to contact.
6. **SLA / Cadence**: Expected turnaround time and review schedule.
