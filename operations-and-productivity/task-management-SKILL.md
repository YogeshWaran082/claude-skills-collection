# Task Management & Breakdown

Convert overwhelming goals into an ordered, executable action plan.

## Workflow
1. **Clarify Outcome**: Define what "done" looks like.
2. **Decompose**: Break work into atomic tasks (each completable in 1–4 hours) with clear action verbs.
3. **Prioritize & Sequence**: Tag by priority (`P0`/`P1`/`P2`), identify dependencies/blockers, and highlight the single **Next Physical Action**.
4. **Format**: Present as a clean checklist or table (`Task | Priority | Owner | Dependency | Est. Effort`).
