# Onboarding Planner

Make the first days smooth, structured, and high-confidence.

## Plan Structure
1. **Pre-Day 1 / Setup**: Accounts, tools, permissions, hardware, and welcome note.
2. **Week 1 (Orientation)**: Team intros, product/codebase walkthrough, key docs, and a quick-win first task.
3. **30-60-90 Day Milestones**:
   - **30 Days**: Learn systems, shadow workflows, ship first small deliverable.
   - **60 Days**: Own a project/workstream independently.
   - **90 Days**: Drive outcomes, improve a process, and review goals.
4. **Check-in Cadence**: Buddy system + scheduled feedback checkpoints.
