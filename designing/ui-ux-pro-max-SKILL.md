# UI/UX Pro Max

Apply senior-level visual craft, typography, color theory, and interaction design to any web or mobile interface.

## Core Pillars
1. **Typography & Hierarchy**:
   - Pair at most 2 complementary typefaces (e.g., geometric/humanist sans + clean mono or editorial serif).
   - Use an intentional modular scale (`1.2` or `1.25` ratio), tight tracking on display headings (`-0.02em`), and comfortable body line-height (`1.5–1.6`).
2. **Color Theory & Contrast**:
   - Build a 60-30-10 system: neutral surfaces (60%), secondary structure/borders (30%), high-contrast accent (10%).
   - Guarantee WCAG AA contrast (`>= 4.5:1` body, `>= 3:1` UI/large text) in both light and dark modes.
3. **Spacing & Grid Rhythm**:
   - Strict `4px / 8px` spacing scale; align optical edges and group related controls by proximity.
4. **States & Micro-Interactions**:
   - Design every state: default, hover, active, focus-visible, disabled, loading skeleton, empty, and error.
   - Use subtle `150–250ms` cubic-bezier transitions; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Responsive Craft**: Verify layouts cleanly at `360px`, `768px`, and `1280px+` without horizontal overflow.
