# Draft Response

Write responses that resolve the issue quickly while building trust.

## Workflow
1. **Acknowledge & Validate**: Show you understood the exact question or frustration without robotic filler.
2. **Answer Directly First**: Lead with the solution, status, or clear next step.
3. **Explain Clearly**: Provide concise, step-by-step instructions (no internal jargon).
4. **Set Expectations**: If unresolved, state exact owner, next update time, and what you need from them.

## Tone
Warm, concise, accountable, and solution-focused. Never blame the user.
