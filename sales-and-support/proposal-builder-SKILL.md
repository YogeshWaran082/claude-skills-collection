# Proposal Builder

Write clear, client-centric proposals that make saying "yes" easy.

## Proposal Structure
1. **Executive Summary**: Mirror the client's goals, pain points, and desired business outcome.
2. **Proposed Solution & Scope**: Clear deliverables, methodology, and explicit **Out of Scope** items.
3. **Timeline & Milestones**: Phased schedule with client dependencies noted.
4. **Investment / Pricing**: Transparent options (e.g., Standard vs. Accelerated) and payment milestones.
5. **Why Us & Next Steps**: Relevant proof points + exact sign-off/kickoff action.
