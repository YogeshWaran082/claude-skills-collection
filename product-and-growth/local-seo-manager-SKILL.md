# Local SEO Manager

Improve local pack rankings and high-intent local organic traffic.

## Checklist
1. **Google Business Profile (GBP)**: Primary/secondary categories, service areas, hours, Q&A, weekly posts, and geo-relevant photos.
2. **On-Page & Location Pages**: City/service-specific H1, title tags, `LocalBusiness` schema markup, embedded map, and unique local proof.
3. **NAP Consistency**: Exact match of Name, Address, Phone across website, GBP, and top local directories.
4. **Reviews & Reputation**: Review generation workflow and response templates (positive + negative).
5. **Local Keywords**: `[service] + [city/near me]` intent mapping without keyword stuffing.
