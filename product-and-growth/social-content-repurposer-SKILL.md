# Social Content Repurposer

Adapt one core idea to fit each platform's native format, hook style, and audience expectations.

## Workflow
1. **Extract Core Angles**: Identify 1 main takeaway, 1 contrarian/surprising insight, and 1 actionable step.
2. **Generate Platform-Native Outputs**:
   - **X / Twitter**: Punchy hook + 3–5 tweet thread or single high-signal post (no hashtag spam).
   - **LinkedIn**: Strong 2-line hook before "See more", scannable spacing, story/lesson, discussion question.
   - **Short-Form Video (Reels/Shorts)**: 3-second visual/spoken hook, 30–45s value beats, clear CTA.
   - **Newsletter Blurb**: Subject line options + 100-word summary + link CTA.
