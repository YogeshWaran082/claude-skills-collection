# PRD & Feature Spec Writer

Align engineering, design, and stakeholders around a crisp, unambiguous spec.

## Sections
1. **Problem & Goal**: Who has the problem, why it matters now, and what success looks like.
2. **Target Users & Use Cases**: Primary persona and core jobs-to-be-done.
3. **Scope**:
   - **In Scope (MVP)**
   - **Out of Scope (Non-goals)** — explicitly prevent scope creep.
4. **User Stories & Acceptance Criteria**: `Given / When / Then` or clear testable checkboxes, including edge & error states.
5. **Metrics & Risks**: Primary KPI, guardrail metrics, dependencies, and open questions.
