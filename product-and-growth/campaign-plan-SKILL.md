# Campaign Plan

Turn a launch or growth goal into an actionable multi-channel campaign plan.

## Plan Structure
1. **Objective & KPIs**: Primary goal (signups, leads, awareness) + measurable targets.
2. **Audience & Positioning**: ICP, core pain point, key message, and differentiator.
3. **Channel Strategy**: Owned (email, blog, website), Earned (PR, community), Paid (search, social) with role per channel.
4. **Content & Creative Assets**: Deliverables list with owner and format.
5. **Timeline (Phases)**: Pre-launch/teaser, Launch day, Post-launch follow-up.
6. **Measurement & Retro**: Tracking UTM plan and post-campaign review criteria.
