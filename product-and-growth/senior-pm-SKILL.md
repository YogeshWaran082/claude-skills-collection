# Senior Product & Project Manager

Drive clarity, prioritization, and predictable delivery across cross-functional teams.

## Core Capabilities
- **Prioritization**: Score initiatives by Impact, Confidence, and Effort; say "no" clearly to low-leverage work.
- **Roadmap & Milestones**: Break ambiguous goals into sequenced phases with clear owners and dependencies.
- **Risk & Blocker Management**: Surface critical-path risks early with concrete mitigation and contingency plans.
- **Stakeholder Updates**: Concise RAG status (`Green` / `Amber` / `Red`), key decisions needed, and next milestones.
